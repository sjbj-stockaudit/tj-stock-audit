# Stock Audit – Guide for Tractor Junction

This is your access to the weekly stock verification of your centres, carried out by M/s SJBJ & Associates. You can view every approved audit with its photos and download the reports at any time.

**Address:** https://tj-stock-audit.sjbjtractor.workers.dev

**Your login:** the username and password shared with you by SJBJ & Associates.

You only see audits after SJBJ has reviewed and approved them.

---

## 1. Opening it

### On a laptop or desktop (best for downloading reports)
Open the address in **Chrome**, **Edge** or **Safari** and sign in. Tip: bookmark it.

### On an Android phone
1. Open the address in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. To add it to your home screen: tap **⋮** (top right) → **Add to Home screen** → **Add**.
3. Open it from the **Stock Audit** icon from now on.

### On an iPhone
1. Open the address in **Safari** and sign in.
2. To add it to your home screen: tap **Share** (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open it from the **Stock Audit** icon from now on.

---

## 2. Stock verification reports (home page)
- Choose the period with **From** / **To**, or tap **This week** / **Last week**.
- The summary shows centres audited, tractors in inventory, **Short** and **Extra**.
- The table lists each approved audit: date, centre, auditor, tractors checked, short, extra and mismatch counts.

## 3. Viewing one centre's audit
Click the centre name. You will see:
- **Counts:** in inventory, verified, short (not found), extra, chassis mismatch
- **Visit details:** auditor, outlet manager, check-in and check-out time, the **storefront photo** and the **auditor's selfie at check-in**
- **Tractors:** registration and chassis from your list next to what the auditor found, the status, remarks and photos. Click a photo to enlarge it – each photo carries the date, time and GPS location
- **Accessories** with quantities and photos
- **Facility condition** – how each area is kept (showroom, yard, accessory racks, battery and tyre storage, cleanliness & safety), rated **Good / Average / Poor**, with photos and remarks
- **Signatures** of the auditor and the outlet manager

## 4. Downloading reports
On a centre's page:
- **Excel** – the stock verification sheet for that centre
- **Download full report** – a zip with the Excel, a PDF of all photos (storefront first, then each tractor captioned by registration number), and the individual photos named by registration number

On the home page, for the whole period:
- Tick the audits you want (all are ticked by default) → **Download combined report**
- You get one zip with a **consolidated Excel** (summary, full inventory with match / mismatch status, one sheet per centre) plus each centre's folder

**Opening the zip:**
- Laptop: double-click the downloaded file.
- Android: open **Files** → **Downloads** → tap the zip → **Extract**.
- iPhone: open **Files** → **Downloads** → tap the zip once to unpack it.

## 5. What the statuses mean
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Tractor present, chassis number matches your records |
| **Chassis Number Mismatch** | Tractor present, but the chassis number differs from your records |
| **Not Found** (short) | In your inventory list, not physically present |
| **Extra – Not in Inventory** | Physically present, not in your inventory list |

Click **Password** (bottom left on a laptop; open **Menu** on a phone) → enter the current and new password → **Change password**.
