# Stock Audit – Auditor Guide

This app is how you do every stock audit: check in at the centre, check each tractor, take photos, collect signatures and submit. Everything you record goes straight to the office – there is no paper sheet or separate GPS camera app any more.

**App address:** https://tj-stock-audit.sjbjtractor.workers.dev

**Your login:** the username and starting password sent to you by the office.

---

## 1. Install the app (one time)

### Android phone (recommended)
1. Open the **Stock Audit** file (`.apk`) the office sent you on WhatsApp.
2. If the phone says installing from this source is not allowed, tap **Settings** → turn on **Allow from this source** → go back → **Install**.
3. If **Play Protect** shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. (This appears because the app is not on the Play Store. It is safe.)
4. Open **Stock Audit** from your home screen.
5. When asked, allow:
   - **Camera** – for the check-in selfie and all photos
   - **Location** – **Allow** (with precise location on)
   - **Notifications** – you will see "Stock audit in progress" while you are checked in.

### iPhone
1. Open **Safari** (not Chrome) and go to the app address above.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open **Stock Audit** from your home screen from now on.
4. When asked, **Allow** camera and location.
5. **Important on iPhone:** keep the app open on screen while you are auditing. iPhones stop location recording when the screen is locked or you switch to another app.

## 2. Sign in

**Hindi:** tap **हिंदी** at the top of the sign-in screen (or in the top bar after signing in) to use the app in Hindi. Tap **English** to switch back.
1. Enter your **username** and **password** → **Sign in**. You stay signed in on your phone.
2. Change your starting password the first time: tap **your name** at the top → enter the current and new password → **Change password**.

### One-time phone setting (Android app)
The first time you open an audit, a blue box asks you to keep location working with the screen off. Tap **Open app settings** → **Battery** → **Unrestricted / No restrictions**, and **Permissions → Location → Allow**. Then come back and tap **Done**. Phones like Xiaomi, Realme, Vivo and Oppo otherwise stop location recording to save battery.

While an audit is open the screen stays on, so it does not lock by itself.

**Phone back button (Android app):** it first closes whatever is open (camera, pop-up), otherwise goes to the previous screen. On the home screen it puts the app in the background – it does not close it, so location recording during an audit keeps running.

## 3. Your centres
The home screen lists:
- **Checked in now** – the audit you are doing
- **Sent back for correction** – an audit the office wants you to fix
- **To audit** – centres assigned to you today
- **Coming up** / **Submitted**

Each centre shows the address, the outlet manager's name and phone, and opening hours. Tap **Directions** to open Google Maps.

## 4. Check in at the centre
1. Tap **Start →** on the centre.
2. Take a **selfie at the centre** (with the showroom behind you if possible) → **Check in**.
3. If you are far from the centre's saved location, the app tells you the distance. If you really are at the centre, tap **Check in anyway** – the office will see the distance.

From check-in until you submit, your location is recorded.

### Storefront photo (required)
Right after check-in, at the top of the audit page: stand outside and tap **Storefront photo** → **Take photo** → **Use photo**. Get the whole showroom front with the **Tractor Junction board** visible. The box turns from yellow to "✓ Taken". You cannot submit without it.

---

## 5. Record every tractor you are shown (Tractors tab)
You do **not** get the stock list. Ask the centre to show you every tractor and record each one:

1. Tap **+ Record a tractor**.
2. Type the **chassis number** exactly as punched on the tractor, the **number plate** (leave empty if there is none), the **engine number** and the **meter reading** (choose **KM** or **Hours**, as shown on the tractor's meter).
3. Tap **Save, then take photos**, then take all 3 photos: **Chassis photo**, **Tractor photo** and **Meter photo**. The numbers must be readable – use **Torch on** in dark places.

If the app says **"Please check the chassis number on the tractor again and type it once more"**, look at the chassis plate again carefully and type the number again (watch 0/O, 1/I, 5/S, 8/B). Type exactly what you see.

Something unusual about a tractor (customer's tractor in for service, demo tractor, chassis plate damaged)? Write it in **Remark (optional)** before saving.

Recorded a tractor by mistake? Tap **Remove** on it.

> Photos can only be taken live in the app – gallery photos cannot be used. Every photo is stamped like a GPS camera photo: a small map with a pin, the address, latitude/longitude, day, date and time. You can take the next photo straight away – photos upload in the background (a spinner shows on the thumbnail; if one says **Retry**, tap it).

---

## 6. Done counting – tractors kept somewhere else
When every tractor shown at the centre is recorded, open the **Finish** tab and tap **Done counting**.

- The app tells you **how many** tractors in the records were **not shown** to you (not which ones).
- Ask the manager where they are. If they are at another yard or a service centre, **go there today** with the centre staff:
  1. On the Finish tab tap **+ Check in at another place**, enter the name of the place, take a photo of it → **Check in here**.
  2. Record each tractor there with **+ Record a tractor** (it is marked at that place automatically).
  3. Come back to **Finish** and tap **Check again**.
- **Everything must be finished the same day.** From the next day no tractors can be added; anything not shown is reported as short.
- If some tractors are still not shown, write the **manager's explanation** (e.g. "1 sold on Monday, invoice pending").
- Photograph every document the manager shows (invoice, delivery challan, gate pass, job card, transfer note) under **Supporting documents** on the Finish tab.

See **section 12** for exactly what to do in each situation.

---

## 7. Accessories (Accessories tab)
For each item (top link, batteries, hood, tyres…):
1. Enter the **Item** and **Qty**, add a remark if needed (e.g. "2 bundles of 5, 2 unpacked") → **Add, then take photo**.
2. Take the photo.

---

## 8. Facility condition (Facility tab)
Shows the client how the centre keeps its stock. For **each area** (showroom display, tractor yard, accessory racks, battery storage, tyre storage, cleanliness & safety):
1. Tap the condition: **Good**, **Average**, **Poor** or **N/A** (area not at this centre).
2. Tap **Photo** → **Take photo** → **Use photo** – show the racks, how goods are arranged and their condition (not needed for N/A).
3. Write a remark – **required for Poor and N/A** (e.g. "batteries kept on the floor near water").

Each area shows "✓ Done" when complete. Use **Add** at the bottom for any other area worth reporting (e.g. workshop).

---

## 9. Manager's declaration and submit (Finish tab)
1. Check the warnings (e.g. "tractor without meter photo", "storefront photo not taken") – they are links, tap to fix.
2. **Manager's declaration** – show the screen to the manager. It says all tractors and accessories were shown, lists the other places and how many were not shown. Check the manager's **name** and **mobile**, and ask them to **sign** → **Save signature**. No printout is needed.
3. Tap **Send the manager a copy on WhatsApp** so the manager has the summary they signed.
4. **Auditor signature** – sign → **Save signature**.
5. Tap **Submit audit and check out**.

After submitting you cannot change the audit unless the office sends it back.

---

## 10. Centre closed or audit not possible
If you reach the centre and the audit cannot be done (shutter down, manager not available, centre refuses, holiday): tap **Centre closed / can't audit?** (below the check-in box, or at the bottom of **Finish**) → choose the **reason**, add details → take a **photo of the storefront** → **Send report**. Your location is recorded as proof. The office reviews it.

---

## 11. If the office sends an audit back
It appears under **Sent back for correction** with a note. Tap **Fix and resubmit →** → **Check in and fix** (no new selfie needed), make the corrections, then submit again from **Finish**.

---

## 12. Audit rules – when the count does not match
You never see the stock list, so you will not know which tractors are "short" or "extra". The app works that out. Your job is to **record exactly what you see, and get proof for everything you don't see**.

### Before you start counting
- Do not tell the centre in advance when you are coming, and do not discuss the stock count with the staff.
- Ask the manager to **stop tractors moving in or out** until you finish. If a tractor arrives or leaves during the count, record it and write that in its remark.
- **Read every chassis plate yourself.** Do not let staff read numbers out to you or type them for you.
- Go area by area (showroom, yard, back side, shed) so nothing is missed or counted twice.

### While counting
- Record **every tractor on the premises**, including used/exchange tractors, demo tractors and customers' tractors in for service. Write what it is in the **remark**, and photograph its paper (e.g. job card) under Supporting documents. The office decides what counts.
- Chassis plate damaged, unreadable, freshly painted, loose or missing? Record what you can read, take a clear chassis photo, write it in the remark, and **call the office**.
- Never change a number to "make it match". If the app asks you to re-check a chassis, look again and type exactly what is punched.

### When the app says tractors were not shown
Ask the manager about **each** missing tractor. Then act on what they say:

| Manager says | What you do |
|---|---|
| At another yard / godown | Go there **today** with the staff, **check in at that place**, record the tractors there |
| At a service centre / workshop | Go there today if it is nearby and record them. If it is too far, photograph the **job card** or service entry and write the address in the explanation |
| Sold / delivered to a customer | Photograph the **sale invoice and delivery challan / gate pass** |
| Transferred to another centre | Photograph the **transfer note**, and write which centre it went to |
| With a customer for a demo or trial | Write the customer's **name, village and phone** in the explanation. If you cannot see the tractor, it stays short |
| No clear answer | Write exactly what was said. **Call the office before you leave** |

- **Never record a tractor you have not seen yourself.** A document is proof of the reason, not a replacement for seeing the tractor. A tractor you did not see stays **short** in the report.
- Write the manager's explanation in their own words, then let them read and sign the declaration.

### Call the office immediately if
- **More than 2 tractors** are not shown, or the manager cannot explain them
- The manager or staff **refuse** the count, rush you, or will not take you to the other yard
- A document or chassis plate looks **altered or suspicious**
- Anyone offers you money, gifts or pressure – report it, do not accept

### Your conduct
- Tea and water are fine; do not accept meals, gifts or favours from the centre.
- Do not share what you found with centre staff or other centres.
- Finish and submit the **same day**.

## Problems?
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Camera permission is blocked" | Phone **Settings → Apps → Stock Audit → Permissions → Camera → Allow**, then **Try again** |
| "Could not get your location" | Turn on GPS/Location, step outside for a moment, try again |
| "No internet connection" | Check mobile data. Your work already saved is safe |
| Forgot password | Ask the office to reset it |
| App looks different | The office updated it – that's normal. Close and reopen the app to get the latest version |
