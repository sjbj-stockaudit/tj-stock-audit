# Stock Audit – Auditor Guide

This app is how you do every stock audit: check in at the centre, check each tractor, take photos, collect signatures and submit. Everything you record goes straight to the office – there is no paper sheet or separate GPS camera app any more.

**App address:** https://tj-stock-audit.sjbjtractor.workers.dev

**Your login:** the username and starting password sent to you by the office.

---

## 1. Install the app (one time)

### Android phone (recommended)
1. Open the **Stock Audit** file (`.apk`) the office sent you on WhatsApp.
2. If the phone says installing from this source is not allowed, tap **Settings** → turn on **Allow from this source** → go back → **Install**.
3. If **Play Protect** shows a warning, tap **More details → Install anyway**. (This appears because the app is not on the Play Store. It is safe.)
4. Open **Stock Audit** from your home screen.
5. When asked, allow:
   - **Camera** – for the check-in selfie and all photos
   - **Location** – **Allow** (with precise location on)
   - **Notifications** – you will see "Stock audit in progress" while you are checked in.

### iPhone
1. Open **Safari** (not Chrome) and go to the app address above.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open **Stock Audit** from your home screen from now on.
4. When asked, **Allow** camera and location.
5. **Important on iPhone:** keep the app open on screen while you are auditing. iPhones stop location recording when the screen is locked or you switch to another app.

---

## 2. Sign in

**Hindi:** tap **हिंदी** at the top of the sign-in screen (or in the top bar after signing in) to use the app in Hindi. Tap **English** to switch back.
1. Enter your **username** and **password** → **Sign in**. You stay signed in on your phone.
2. Change your starting password the first time: tap **your name** at the top → enter the current and new password → **Change password**.

### One-time phone setting (Android app)
The first time you open an audit, a blue box asks you to keep location working with the screen off. Tap **Open app settings** → **Battery** → **Unrestricted / No restrictions**, and **Permissions → Location → Allow**. Then come back and tap **Done**. Phones like Xiaomi, Realme, Vivo and Oppo otherwise stop location recording to save battery.

While an audit is open the screen stays on, so it does not lock by itself.

---

## 3. Your centres
The home screen lists:
- **Checked in now** – the audit you are doing
- **Sent back for correction** – an audit the office wants you to fix
- **To audit** – centres assigned to you today
- **Coming up** / **Submitted**

Each centre shows the address, the outlet manager's name and phone, and opening hours. Tap **Directions** to open Google Maps.

---

## 4. Check in at the centre
1. Tap **Start →** on the centre.
2. Take a **selfie at the centre** (with the showroom behind you if possible) → **Check in**.
3. If you are far from the centre's saved location, the app tells you the distance. If you really are at the centre, tap **Check in anyway** – the office will see the distance.

From check-in until you submit, your location is recorded.

### Storefront photo (required)
Right after check-in, at the top of the audit page: stand outside and tap **Storefront photo** → **Take photo** → **Use photo**. Get the whole showroom front with the **Tractor Junction board** visible. The box turns from yellow to "✓ Taken". You cannot submit without it.

---

## 5. Check every tractor (Stock tab)
Tap a tractor in the list. For each one:

1. **Photo of the chassis number** – tap **Chassis photo** → **Take photo** → **Use photo** (or **Retake**). Use **Torch on** in dark places. The number must be readable.
2. **Photo of the whole tractor** – tap **Tractor photo** → **Take photo** → **Use photo**.
3. **Type the chassis number** exactly as punched on the chassis. The app tells you if it matches the stock list.
4. Check the **RC / registration number**.
5. Choose the result:
   - **✓ Verified** – tractor present, chassis matches
   - **Mismatch** – tractor present but the chassis number is different (write a remark)
   - **Not found** – the tractor in the list is not at the centre (no photos needed)

The next tractor opens automatically. Use the search box to find a tractor by registration, chassis or engine number.

> Photos can only be taken live in the app – gallery photos cannot be used. Every photo is stamped like a GPS camera photo: a small map with a pin, the address, latitude/longitude, day, date and time. You can take the next photo straight away – photos upload in the background (a spinner shows on the thumbnail; if one says **Retry**, tap it).

---

## 6. Extra tractors (Extra tab)
For tractors at the centre that are **not in the stock list**:
1. Enter the **Registration no.** and **Chassis no.** → **Add, then take photos**.
2. Take the **chassis photo** and the **tractor photo**.

---

## 7. Accessories (Accessories tab)
For each item (top link, batteries, hood, tyres…):
1. Enter the **Item** and **Qty**, add a remark if needed (e.g. "2 bundles of 5, 2 unpacked") → **Add, then take photo**.
2. Take the photo.

---

## 8. Facility condition (Facility tab)
Shows the client how the centre keeps its stock. For **each area** (showroom display, tractor yard, accessory racks, battery storage, tyre storage, cleanliness & safety):
1. Tap the condition: **Good**, **Average**, **Poor** or **N/A** (area not at this centre).
2. Tap **Photo** → **Take photo** → **Use photo** – show the racks, how goods are arranged and their condition (not needed for N/A).
3. Write a remark – **required for Poor and N/A** (e.g. "batteries kept on the floor near water").

Each area shows "✓ Done" when complete. Use **Add** at the bottom for any other area worth reporting (e.g. workshop).

---

## 9. Finish and submit (Finish tab)
1. Check the summary. Any warnings (e.g. "tractor without photo", "storefront photo not taken") are links – tap to fix.
2. **Auditor signature** – sign with your finger → **Save signature**.
3. **Outlet manager** – check the manager's name, ask them to sign → **Save signature**.
4. Tap **Submit audit and check out**.

After submitting you cannot change the audit unless the office sends it back.

---

## 10. Centre closed or audit not possible
If you reach the centre and the audit cannot be done (shutter down, manager not available, centre refuses, holiday): tap **Centre closed / can't audit?** (below the check-in box, or at the bottom of **Finish**) → choose the **reason**, add details → take a **photo of the storefront** → **Send report**. Your location is recorded as proof. The office reviews it.

---

## 11. If the office sends an audit back
It appears under **Sent back for correction** with a note. Tap **Fix and resubmit →** → **Check in and fix** (no new selfie needed), make the corrections, then submit again from **Finish**.

---

## Problems?
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Camera permission is blocked" | Phone **Settings → Apps → Stock Audit → Permissions → Camera → Allow**, then **Try again** |
| "Could not get your location" | Turn on GPS/Location, step outside for a moment, try again |
| "No internet connection" | Check mobile data. Your work already saved is safe |
| Forgot password | Ask the office to reset it |
| App looks different | The office updated it – that's normal. Close and reopen the app to get the latest version |
