# Stock Audit – Planner Guide

You plan the stock audits: **which centre is audited on which date, and by which auditor**. The office checks your plan, approves it and sends it to the client, who then sends the stock list for each day's centres.

**Address:** https://tj-stock-audit.sjbjtractor.workers.dev

**Your login:** the username and password given to you by the office. Change the password the first time: **Password** (bottom left on a laptop; **Menu** on a phone).

---

## Plan a week
1. Sign in – you land on **Weekly plan**, showing **next week** (Monday to Sunday). Use **←** / **→** or the date box to change the week.
2. For each day, choose a centre in **Add a centre…**, choose the **Auditor…** (their base area is shown in brackets) → **Add**.
3. Change the auditor or add **Notes** on any line at any time; **Remove** takes a centre off that day.

### Checks shown at the top
- **Things to check** – centres planned twice in the week, a centre planned on its weekly off day, an auditor with more than 2 centres on one day, or a centre without an auditor.
- **Centres not planned this week** – listed at the bottom of the page.

## When the plan is final
Tell the office. They **Approve & lock** the week – the badge changes from **Draft** to **Approved**, and you can no longer change it. If something changes after that, ask the office to unlock it.

**Download plan (Excel)** gives the plan as a sheet (with both logos) if you need to share it.

## Following the week
Each planned line shows the visit's progress: **Assigned** (stock list received, auditor can start) → **In progress** → **Awaiting review** → **Approved**. "Waiting for stock statement" means the client's list for that day hasn't been uploaded yet. "Not carried out" means the auditor reported the centre closed.

You do not see audit photos, reports or staff locations – those stay with the office.
